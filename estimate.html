Before you send

Quiet answers to fair questions

Is this a quote? Am I committing to anything?
No. An estimate is arithmetic, not an order — nothing is sourced, nothing is reserved, and nothing is charged until you approve the final quote I confirm personally.
What happens after I press send?
Your summary arrives to me through the form, and a copy sits on your clipboard as a backup. I reply within a day with the real store price, current availability, and exact shipping for your address — that reply is the final quote.
When do I actually pay?
Only after you approve the final quote, and in two parts: a deposit covering your pieces and fees so I can buy them, then the shipping balance once your parcel is packed and weighed — so shipping is the real courier price, not a guess. You are never asked to pay from this page.
Is paying safe?
Bank transfer, Wise, PayPal, or Payoneer — choose PayPal Goods & Services for buyer protection. You receive photo confirmation of every piece before it ships, and a tracking number the moment it does.
Why is shipping such a large share of the total?
Couriers price clothes by weight and volume, and honest international rates are simply high — a recent 0.5–1 kg parcel ran 1,950,000₫. Shipping is passed through at exact cost, never marked up, and I repack carefully to cut the volume you would otherwise pay for.
What if it doesn't fit, or arrives wrong?
Sizing is confirmed against the real garment's measurements before we buy — that is part of the service. If the wrong item arrives or it is damaged, tell me within 48 hours with an unboxing video and I'll make it right.
