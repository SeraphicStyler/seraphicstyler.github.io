Good to know

Shipping & duties

  • Shipping is the courier’s actual cost on the day it ships, including any fuel or peak-season surcharges. It’s quoted before you pay it.
  • Import duties and taxes in your country are yours.

Minimums

  • The buying fee is at least $14 per piece and $25 per buy, so very inexpensive pieces may cost a little more than 15%.

What scouting covers

  • Three to four hours of finding designers, and a line sheet of options with photos, prices and sizes.
  • It isn’t deducted from your order, and the line sheet is yours even if you don’t buy.

You’re in control

  • Nothing is bought without your yes. Your exact numbers come in a written quote first.
