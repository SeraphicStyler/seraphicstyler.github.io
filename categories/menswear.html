Menswear

13 Vietnamese menswear houses, each verified in person across Ho Chi Minh City and beyond.

How to read the data. A listing records what was found in field research and the house's own channels; it is not a promise of live stock or opening hours. Saigon labels move often, so confirm before travelling. Read the methodology and correction policy.

  • AristinoL2-17, Vincom Đồng Khởi, 72 Lê Thánh Tôn · Menswear · Walk-in store — K&G's premium men's brand — sharp shirts, polos & suiting-adjacent smart-casual; 30+ HCMC showrooms (Crescent Mall, Vincom Gò Vấp, D3/D5/Tân Bình). Mall showrooms 9:00–22:00.
  • Bad Habits22 Lê Thị Riêng + 42 Tôn Thất Thiệp + TNP · Menswear · Walk-in store — Saigon unisex streetwear (est. 2017) with Vietnamese-culture graphics; 4th door at 2 Trần Huy Liệu, Phú Nhuận. Also Hanoi, Cần Thơ & Bangkok.
  • Cao Minh Saigon74/6 Hai Bà Trưng · Menswear · By appointment — Vietnam's oldest tailoring house (est. 1948) — the prestige choice; heritage atelier villa at 193/13 Điện Biên Phủ, Bình Thạnh by appointment. ~9:00–18:00, reservations recommended.
  • Degrey24 Hồ Biểu Chánh · Menswear · Walk-in store — Gen-Z streetwear mixing Vietnamese cultural motifs; also 67–69 Đường D10 (Tân Phú) + 382D Trần Hưng Đạo (D5 · Chợ Lớn).
  • DirtyCoinsThe New Playground, 26 Lý Tự Trọng · Menswear · Walk-in store — Arguably Vietnam's biggest streetwear name — heavy branding, hip-hop-adjacent. Destination flagship at 561 Sư Vạn Hạnh (D10); also TNP @ Vincom Đồng Khởi, Grand Park & Aeon Bình Tân. 9:00–22:00.
  • emdash7 Đường số 1 · Menswear · Walk-in store — Cuddle's menswear line (2023) — linen shirts, trousers, sets. Same Thảo Điền door.
  • H&D TailorStore No. 5, New World Saigon Hotel arcade, 76 Lê Lai · Menswear · Walk-in store — Third-generation master tailor Tạ Quốc Hưng — an expat & business-traveler standby for 20 years; tropical-weight wools a specialty. Mon–Sat ~9:00–19:00.
  • HADES StudioB1 Vincom Đồng Khởi + 152 Trần Quang Diệu · Menswear · Walk-in store — First-wave VN streetwear — darker, grunge-leaning design language. (Older lists citing Gò Vấp/Phạm Ngũ Lão branches are stale.)
  • ICONDENIM12–12bis Cách Mạng Tháng 8 + 261 Nguyễn Trãi · Menswear · Walk-in store — Denim-first men's casual chain — one of HCMC's largest local menswear retailers; 13 city stores (D7, Tân Phú, Bình Thạnh…). 8:30–22:00.
  • Mêman26B–C Lê Lợi · Menswear · Walk-in store — Minimalist menswear & modern áo dài in Japanese linen. 11:00–20:00.
  • Môi Điên28 Tôn Thất Thiệp · Menswear · Walk-in store — Tom Trandt's cult experimental label — deconstructed tailoring, upcycling, unisex statement pieces. Call ahead ☎ 090 395 12 12.
  • Routine523A Quang Trung · Menswear · Walk-in store — Menswear-rooted, now VN's benchmark for office-to-weekend basics; 1,000 m² keystone store (May 2025) + Aeon Bình Tân & Tân Phú Celadon malls. 9:00–22:00 daily. See also Routine U Space (Q10) for the two-floor concept version.
  • SIR Tailor207F Nguyễn Văn Thủ · Menswear · By appointment — Modern gentleman's atelier since 2013 — full-canvas suits from 7.9M₫ (Asian cloth) / 16.9M₫ (Italian). Fittings by appointment; 21–30 day turnaround, minimum two fittings.
