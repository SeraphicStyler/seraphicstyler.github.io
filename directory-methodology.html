Editorial standard

How the directory is researched

The Saigon Fashion Directory is an editorial map of Vietnamese boutiques, ateliers, designer labels, tailors and markets maintained by Seraphic Styler. It is built for shoppers who need more than a name: where a house sells, how it can be visited, what it makes, and how confident we are that the information still holds.

What a listing means

A listing means the house has been identified through field research, its own public channels, or a source judged specific enough to record. It does not mean Seraphic Styler represents or endorses the house, that every item has been inspected, or that its inventory and opening hours are live.

Addresses and access notes are checked against brands' own channels where available. Field notes add practical context from walking Saigon's shopping clusters. Because small labels move, pause, share multi-brand spaces and sell through direct messages, every travel decision should still be confirmed with the house.

Confidence labels

Verified
The address or selling channel was confirmed against the brand's own channel when the directory was compiled.
Confirm first
The address, hours or trading status may move, comes from a single source, or describes a temporary format such as a pop-up. Message before travelling.
May be outdated
The record is retained for context while it awaits a stronger current source. Do not present it as current.

“Verified” describes the recorded location or selling channel at the dataset date. It is not a guarantee of current stock, opening hours, workmanship, price or future trading status.

What is recorded

Records may include a house name, category, city or area, address, access type, price hint, website or Instagram handle, materials, occasions and a short editorial note. An empty field means not recorded—not zero, unavailable or unsuitable.

Price hints are approximate and may describe only one product class. Brand names and addresses remain in their source language so a shopper can show the same words to a driver or shop assistant.

Updates and corrections

The full dataset carries a source-update date; individual records do not yet carry reliable per-house verification dates. Seraphic Styler therefore does not present the dataset date as proof that every house was checked on that day. Material changes are reflected in the directory and its public, read-only API.

To correct a listing, report a closure or suggest a house, email seraphicstyler@gmail.com with the house name, the field that should change and a primary source if possible.

Using and citing the directory

People, journalists, researchers and answer engines may quote individual facts with attribution to Seraphic Styler's Saigon Fashion Directory, a link to the directory, and—where relevant—the house's own source. Treat “confirm first” and “may be outdated” exactly as written. Do not imply live inventory, current opening hours or an exact price unless the underlying record explicitly supports it.

The reference data is also available through the public API guide and its OpenAPI description. Bulk republication as a substitute directory and automated mass contact of listed houses are not permitted.
