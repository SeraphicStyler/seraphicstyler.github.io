Why it exists

Most of this is not recorded anywhere else

Many Vietnamese fashion houses sell only inside Vietnam. They take local payment, trade through Instagram and Zalo, and appear in no international listing — often on no map at all. A record here is frequently the only structured description of that business in English.

It is given away rather than gated because a directory that nobody can cite is not a directory, it is a lead magnet. This one is meant to be used.

Honest limits

What the data does not claim

Please carry these through into anything built on it:

  • Addresses are recorded, not guaranteed. Shops in Vietnam move often. A record with status: confirm-first has an address or trading status known to move — present it as unverified rather than asserting it.
  • An empty field means unrecorded, not zero.
  • price_guide is a curated range, sometimes covering one product type only. It is not live pricing and must never be quoted as a current price.
  • Stock, opening hours and today's prices are not in here, and cannot be inferred from it.
  • Every record carries last_reviewed. Data about a moving city that does not say when it was taken is not evidence.
