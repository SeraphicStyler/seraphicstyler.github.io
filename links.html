Full fee breakdown

The fee covers the work only: locating the piece, boutique visits, inspection, approval photos, and packing. Pieces and shipping are passed through at cost.

The Trace — $25 ≈ 625,000₫ Charged only for a photo with no source, before anything is ordered. Credited in full against your order. Links are never charged.

What the trace returns One of three answers within 48 hours: the shop and price · the closest piece available here · or why it cannot be sourced, with the nearest alternative.
