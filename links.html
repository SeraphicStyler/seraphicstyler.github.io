A wardrobe, considered as a whole: 15–20 selection targets across occasions and Saigon labels.

  • Style profile, wardrobe plan, and digital lookbook
  • Sizing checks and approval photos
  • Ordering coordination and 60 days of post-order support

Custom Wardrobe+ is for 21–30+ pieces or more complex work. Labels, fittings, refinement rounds, destinations, and support are agreed in your quote.

  • Custom Wardrobe includes two refinement rounds before ordering and one consolidated shipment to one address.
  • Extra styling is $25 per added item, plus its store price. Larger scope changes, extra destinations, and extensive alteration support are quoted separately.
  • Rush projects add $100–$150, confirmed before booking.
  • A nonrefundable styling deposit secures the project and counts toward your quoted fee. Clothing credit is spent only on approved purchases.

Fees & payment details
  • Per-item sourcing fee 8% of the piece (7% above 5,000,000₫), minimum 350,000₫ per item
  • Per-order coordination 250,000₫ flat — transport, boutique visits and packing
  • Typical minimum 600,000₫ ≈ $24 before the piece and shipping
  • Optional Rare-find or rush sourcing +200,000₫ · beyond two boutiques +150,000₫ per stop · green shopping takes 10% off the sourcing fees
  • Always Pieces and shipping at cost, no markup
  • Payment costs Card payments carry Stripe’s ~5.4% + $0.30, itemized · bank transfer, Wise and Zelle carry no card fee · a 3% allowance covers currency transfer into Vietnam, at cost

When you need The Trace
  • US$25 per item for identification research, credited toward an order if you proceed.
  • An identified source and available price, or an explanation, within 48 hours after scope confirmation and payment. A match is not guaranteed.
  • Shared fees for closely related items need advance confirmation. More than five research items, alternatives, and wider selection require a styling quote.

Terms

No-surprises pricing — pieces at cost, nothing bought without approval
  • Pieces at the receipt price — no markup
  • Shipping at the carrier's cost, billed once the parcel is weighed
  • Paid in stages — a deposit first, then the actual shipping once the parcel is weighed
  • Bank transfer, Wise and Zelle carry no card fee · cards carry Stripe's ~5.4% + $0.30, itemized · a 3% allowance covers transfer into Vietnam, at cost
  • The exact total is confirmed in writing before any payment
  • Nothing is bought without your approval
