Foundations

Positioning & essence

Seraphic Styler is a bilingual, on-the-ground personal shopper who sources, photographs and ships any Vietnamese fashion brand worldwide — with transparent pricing and a couture eye. Not a reseller, not a forwarder: a stylist who happens to live in Saigon.

“Seraphic” — angelic, ethereal, luminous. The taste is celestial; the service is grounded and honest.
Essence

Ethereal, but honest

Soft, romantic, quietly modern — never loud, never salesy. Elegance reads as restraint.

Difference

Verified in person

Real photos before you pay. She styles and confirms; she doesn’t just forward a link.

Promise

Rare Saigon, delivered

Labels that never leave the city — chosen with care and shipped to your door.

SeraphicStyler

Stacked wordmark

SERAPHIC (Tenor Sans, cobalt, wide-tracked caps) over Styler (Pinyon Script, soft violet).

◆ Do

  • Recolour by the stroke: cobalt #2e54ad on light, ivory on dark, or currentColor
  • Keep clear space of ≥ the mark’s width around it
  • Use the Angelic Aura avatar (assets/social/avatar.svg) for profile photos

◆ Don’t

  • Never an S-in-disc monogram / lettermark / seal (both retired)
  • Don’t stretch, rotate, add drop-shadows or recolour the gradient
  • Don’t place the cobalt mark on a busy mid-tone photo without a scrim

Identity

The Angelic Aura

The signature that makes the brand feel celestial. Use sparingly — it’s the 10%, the glow at the edges, never the whole room.

Halo headings

Headings carry a soft white/lavender text-shadow (the --halo token) so they seem lit from within.

Aurora wash

A blurred lavender·mauve·periwinkle radial fixed behind the page at ~40% opacity. Gossamer, never sharp.

Hero aura bloom

A pearl-white radial bloom behind the hero line; a slow 7s pulse. In dark mode it becomes a dim moon-glow.

Sparkles ✦ ✦

A few twinkling ✦ marks appear after content settles, at ~0.6 opacity. Restraint — two or three, not a shower.

Glass frames

Sections sit in frosted glass cards: backdrop-filter: blur(7px) saturate(130%) with a hairline border and a soft halo shadow.

Pinyon first-letter

Every section title opens with a cursive cobalt initial — the through-line across the whole site.

All aura effects respect prefers-reduced-motion and are removed in high-contrast mode.

Identity

Imagery

◆ Do

  • Real photos — your own try-ons, sourcing trips, flat-lays
  • Soft daylight, airy negative space, ice/lavender surroundings
  • Show the piece honestly: fabric, fit, the tag, the price
  • Sign with a corner butterfly watermark at ~70% opacity

◆ Don’t

  • No watermarked third-party stock or scraped brand images
  • No heavy filters, harsh HDR, or oversaturated colour
  • No busy collages — one calm subject per frame

Overlay kit (transparent PNGs) lives in the archive — titles, lower-thirds, seals, frames, sparkles, CTA pills — in -cobalt and -ivory colourways. Use ivory on dark/busy footage, cobalt on pale.

System

Motion

Motion settles rather than performs. Content arrives; it doesn’t bounce.

  • Reveal — elements fade + rise 10px on scroll, 0.8s ease. The hero choreographs eyebrow → headline → lead → CTAs in ~120ms steps.
  • Hover lift — cards & buttons rise 2px with a gentle cubic-bezier(.25,1,.5,1).
  • Twinkle & aura-pulse — slow, 4–7s loops at low opacity. Ambient, not attention-grabbing.
  • Reduced motion — prefers-reduced-motion and the manual toggle strip all animation; reveals snap to visible.

Expression

Voice & tone

A warm, honest friend in Saigon with a couture eye — generous, specific, never pushy.

Five traits

  • Warm — you genuinely care; a little 🤍 is your signature.
  • Honest — you say when something won’t fit, sells out, or isn’t worth it. Honesty is the brand.
  • Specific — real names, real prices, real photos. Never vague hype.
  • Calm & unpushy — you advise, you don’t sell. No urgency tricks.
  • Quietly elegant — soft, editorial, a touch couture. Lowercase warmth, not loud sales.
