A note on references and exact copies

Seraphic Styler does not reproduce, tailor, generate, or guarantee exact copies of reference designs. Vague descriptions, broad categories, unknown sellers, unlinked inspiration images, and “find something like this” requests do not qualify as direct sourcing.

The clothing credit, explained

A budget toward pieces you love.

For example, the US$249 Capsule booking includes a US$75 styling fee and US$174 toward approved clothes. Shipping is separate. Your credit is a clothing budget, not an additional fee.

How many garments will my credit cover?

Piece ranges are curation targets, subject to fit, inventory, and real store prices. Clothing credit does not guarantee a fixed number of purchased garments. Any additional clothing budget is agreed with you before purchase.
