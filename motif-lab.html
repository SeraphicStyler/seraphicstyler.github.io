Motif Lab

Six Vietnamese-fashion motifs in the house line — one continuous stroke of cobalt ink, drawn on slowly, like thread. Refresh the page to replay.

The Áo Dài

tà áo dài

The signature silhouette — high collar, diagonal closure, panels in motion. For the hero or About.

The Frog Clasp

khuy vải

The knotted silk button that closes an áo dài — a quiet emblem of handwork. Lovely as a small section ornament.

The Lotus

hoa sen

Vietnam's flower, above still water. For the trust or sustainability moment — calm, unhurried.

Silk in Motion

lụa

A ribbon of Vietnamese silk mid-air — movement without a body. A divider between sections, or behind a headline.

The Stitch

đường kim mũi chỉ

Needle, thread, and a running hem — made-to-measure, by hand. For sizing and the ateliers.

Delicate Care

nâng niu

Tissue paper rising from an open parcel, a sprig laid across it — how every order leaves Saigon. For packing, shipping, gifts.

How these would live on the site

  • One per section, never two. They are punctuation, not wallpaper.
  • Drawn on as the section scrolls into view (same animation as here), then still.
  • Cobalt ink on ivory at ~120–150 px; the lavender accent stroke stays rare.
  • They share a hand with the inline butterfly — same stroke weight, same restraint.
