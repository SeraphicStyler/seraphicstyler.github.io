In briefNo tracking cookies; your settings stay on your device.

This information stays on your device, is never sent to me, and can be cleared at any time in your browser settings.

  2. 8.2

  4. 8.4

    Because there is no tracking to opt out of, Global Privacy Control and Do-Not-Track signals require no further action, and are respected in any case.
