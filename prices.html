Boutiques

$250 + 15%scouting, then 15% of the pieces

I scout Vietnamese designers for you, negotiate stockist prices, check every piece and ship it all in one parcel. Pieces are at the designer’s price, never marked up.

  • Scouting fee: $250 per round. Three to four hours of finding designers and building you a line sheet of options. Paid before I start, and it’s mine to keep, like a styling fee. If you don’t buy, the line sheet is still yours.
  • Buying fee: 15% of the pieces you choose, at every size. That’s the same rate as group orders. Rush or made-to-measure is 20% instead.
  • Every buy pays at least $14 per piece and $25 in total in buying fee, the same minimum as buying a single piece.
  • Research before you know what you want: $45 an hour, two-hour minimum, prepaid.

True for every service

  • Nothing is bought without your yes. You see the photo, price and size first.
  • Your price is confirmed in writing before you pay. That written quote is what you pay.
  • Shipping is the courier’s actual cost on the day it ships, including the fuel and peak-season surcharges couriers add (high this autumn). It’s quoted before you pay for it. Import duties and taxes in your country are yours.
  • Paying: bank transfer, Zelle and Wise have no card fee. A card adds about 5.4% + $0.30, which is the processor’s fee, shown before you pay. Orders for pieces also carry the 3% currency transfer. How payment works.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Shop prices in đồng are converted at 25,000₫ = $1.
  • If a piece sells out, see returns & credit.
