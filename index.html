Sizing & fit

Saigon sizing, interpreted

Vietnamese sizing runs small — a Western medium often reads XL here. Real garment measurements are checked before anything is bought.

How to measure at home

I know which Saigon ateliers cut generously, which labels carry true European or extended sizing, and I check every order against the brand’s own chart before buying. Whether you wear a US small or a 5XL, sizing is settled before we commit.

The fine print — petite sizing, fabric & extended labels

VN XS / VN S — the smallest local sizes, for the most petite frames (≈ US 00–0 · UK 2–4 · EU 30–32). If a US XS runs loose on you, start here; otherwise begin at VN M.

Fabric matters. Cotton from a young local brand can run two sizes smaller than linen from a European-leaning atelier. Áo dài and tailored cuts sit close. I confirm real garment measurements before we buy.

Extended sizing, covered. Himistore (to 6XL, EU measurements), curvy boutiques like Cow's House, and Saigon designers who cut M–5XL for international frames. You never guess alone.

FAQ

FAQ
How do I get the sizing right?
Tell me your usual measurements and I'll check each brand's size chart for you. Vietnamese sizing often runs small, so I'll flag it honestly. The final choice is yours, but I'll give you my best read before we order.
Who pays customs and duties?
Any customs duties, taxes, or import fees are paid by the recipient and aren't included in your total. Since 2025 the US applies import duties to every parcel regardless of value; most other countries tax only above a threshold. I declare parcels honestly and tell you what to expect for your destination before you pay your balance — the final figure always belongs to your customs office, not to me.
How long does shipping take?
Roughly 3 to 21 days, depending on your region and the service you choose. East & SE Asia is fastest; rest of world is slowest. You get a tracking number once your parcel ships.
Are the items authentic?
Yes. I buy directly from Vietnamese brands and boutiques in person, here in Saigon. You're getting the real local piece, sourced at the source — never counterfeits.
What if it doesn't fit?
I'm a personal shopper, so a wrong size chosen at order can't be returned — that's why I help you get sizing right beforehand. If you'd like, I can ask the boutique about exchange options before we buy.
Is payment safe?
Yes. Pay by bank transfer, Wise, PayPal, or Payoneer — choose PayPal Goods & Services for buyer protection. USDC (a regulated stablecoin) is available on request, and if none of these reach you easily, tell me and I'll suggest a reputable option for your country. A deposit covering your pieces and fees places the order, then the shipping balance before your parcel goes out — weighed first, so it is the real courier price.
