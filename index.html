About

Your stylist in Saigon

Seraphic Styler is a private styling and sourcing atelier based in Ho Chi Minh City, for those who hold beauty, fashion, and contemporary culture in high regard. I find Vietnamese pieces that feel genuinely singular and bring them to you through a process that is exacting, relaxed, and deeply personal.

My story

Saigon's labels carry a particular romance: modern, quietly elegant, and largely invisible beyond the city. Friends abroad fell in love with that sensibility but had no way to reach it. I began sourcing for them — and that remains the soul of this work: helping you wear a considered piece of Vietnam — crafted with intent and chosen with a cultivated eye.

How I work

You share a brand, a reference, or simply the mood and your size. I source in person, photograph the exact piece, and advise on fit, proportion, and finish before we decide to buy. What you see is precisely what arrives — honestly priced, carefully packed, and guided with discretion from Saigon to your door.

Sizing & fit

Saigon sizing, interpreted

In Vietnam, sizing is built on a different frame: petite and slender are the starting point. A Western medium often arrives labelled XL, and a US large can read as 2XL or 3XL. That moment of surprise when you see the tag is simply a translation issue, never a verdict on your body.

Reading that landscape is part of what I do. I know which Saigon ateliers cut generously, which labels carry true European or extended sizing, and where a piece will sit beautifully without losing its character. Whether you wear a US small or a 5XL, I find options made for your shape as much as your taste.

Your Usual US SizeApprox. Vietnam LabelGood to Know
XS–S · 0–6M–LThe standard range in most Saigon boutiques, so the selection is widest here. Expect cropped lengths, narrower shoulders, and higher armholes — I flag anything that runs especially small or short, and note what can be tailored.
M · 8–10XL–2XLOften the largest size a standard shop carries. Some labels grade up faithfully; others simply add width — I check shoulder seams, sleeve length, and rise in person.
L · 12–142XL–3XLLimited in general stores, so I source from labels that truly pattern for this range rather than merely enlarging a sample.
XL+ · 16+3XL–5XL+Through specialist, size-inclusive labels — Himistore (to 6XL, European measurements), Cow's House, and designers cutting M–5XL for international frames.
Shoes US 8–11EU 39–44Local lasts run narrow; wider fits are sourced.
The fine print — petite sizing, fabric & extended labels

VN XS / VN S — the smallest local sizes, for the most petite frames (≈ US 00–0 · UK 2–4 · EU 30–32). If a US XS runs loose on you, start here; otherwise begin at VN M.

Fabric matters. Cotton from a young local brand can run two sizes smaller than linen from a European-leaning atelier. Áo dài and tailored cuts sit close. I confirm real garment measurements before we buy.

Extended sizing, covered. Himistore (to 6XL, EU measurements), curvy boutiques like Cow's House, and Saigon designers who cut M–5XL for international frames. You never guess alone.

Quick convert: UK ≈ US + 4 · EU ≈ US + 32 · AU ≈ US + 4. (XS–S ≈ UK 4–10 / EU 32–38.)

FAQ
How do I get the sizing right?
Tell me your usual measurements and I'll check each brand's size chart for you. Vietnamese sizing often runs small, so I'll flag it honestly. The final choice is yours, but I'll give you my best read before we order.
Who pays customs and duties?
Any customs duties, taxes, or import fees are paid by the recipient and aren't included in your total. They depend on your country's rules. I declare parcels accurately and can't predict or cover these charges.
How long does shipping take?
Roughly 3 to 21 days, depending on your region and the service you choose. East & SE Asia is fastest; rest of world is slowest. You get a tracking number once your parcel ships.
Are the items authentic?
Yes. I buy directly from Vietnamese brands and boutiques in person, here in Saigon. You're getting the real local piece, sourced at the source — never counterfeits.
What if it doesn't fit?
I'm a personal shopper, so a wrong size chosen at order can't be returned — that's why I help you get sizing right beforehand. If you'd like, I can ask the boutique about exchange options before we buy.
Is payment safe?
Yes. Pay by bank transfer, Wise, PayPal, or Payoneer — choose PayPal Goods & Services for buyer protection. USDC (a regulated stablecoin) is available on request, and if none of these reach you easily, tell me and I'll suggest a reputable option for your country. 50% deposit to order, then 50% plus shipping before your parcel goes out. I send photo confirmation of every item before shipping.
What can you source?
Any Vietnamese local brand or boutique, including pieces not posted here — fashion and beauty: áo dài-modern, coquette, soft-feminine, vintage, skincare & cosmetics. Send a link or screenshot and I'll find it.
Can I return an item?
Only if the wrong item was sent or it arrived damaged. Contact me within 48 hours with a full unboxing video, item unused with tags; return shipping is yours. Not accepted: minor loose threads, slight color difference, light odor, wrong size chosen, or fading after washing.
Do you ship to my country?
Almost certainly yes — I ship worldwide. Not sure about yours? Message me and I'll confirm.
Policies & deposits

Payment & deposit

Bank transfer, Wise, PayPal, or Payoneer; USDC available on request. 50% deposit to place your order; 50% balance + shipping before it ships. Processed within 2–7 days of deposit. Because each order is sourced specifically for you, confirmed purchases are final.

Returns & refunds

I purchase and ship on your behalf and send photo confirmation first. Returns only if the wrong item was sent or it arrived damaged: contact within 48h with a full unboxing video, unused with tags; return shipping is yours. Bespoke styling work is non-refundable once it has begun.

Sourcing limitations

I can source almost any Vietnamese brand, but I do not hold stock and local availability changes quickly. I confirm an item is in stock before taking final payment. If it sells out, I will tell you immediately and suggest the closest alternative.

Shipping timing

Worldwide, typically 3–21 days by region and service. You receive a tracking number once sent. I cannot control delays caused by brands, carriers, or customs.

Customs responsibility

Customs duties, taxes, or import fees are paid by the recipient and are not included in your quote. Rules vary by country — please check your local thresholds. I always declare parcels honestly.

Authenticity

I source directly from official brand stores and trusted local boutiques — never counterfeits. What you order is the genuine local-brand piece, and I'm happy to share where each item came from.

Serious orders & deposits

Higher-touch services — in-shop try-ons, complex sourcing, rush requests — require a small deposit before I begin. It simply holds dedicated time for you and is applied to your final total. Deposits are generally non-refundable once planning or sourcing has started; with 48 hours' notice I am always glad to reschedule once. If a request grows significantly larger than first described, I will gently quote any extra before continuing.

What can vary

Exact fit across brands, stock by order time, shipping/customs outcomes, finding a sold-out piece again, or unlimited revisions. What I do promise: a photograph of every piece before it ships, an honest read on fit, and tracking once it is on its way.

Before you buy

Colours vary across screens. Brand sizing is inconsistent. Limited pieces can sell out before payment clears. Carriers can delay parcels. Customs depends on your destination. Inspiration photographs guide the look — not an identical piece.

Requests I may not be able to take on

Unrealistic rush deadlines I cannot meet with care, anything counterfeit, and anything unsafe or unethical. If a request is not a fit, I will tell you kindly and point you to a better option where I can.
Style notes

Why Vietnam brands

A point of view you rarely find elsewhere: fit cut for real bodies, fair prices close to the source, and quiet craft — clean seams, soft natural fabrics. Modern áo dài, soft coquette, linen minimal, Saigon vintage. Most sell only inside Vietnam, in VND — that gap is what I close.

Who I style best

I work especially well with romantic, coquette, minimal, workwear, occasion, and vintage wardrobes — or a thoughtful mix. Unsure where you sit? Send a few photographs you love and I will read the thread between them.

What to send me

Links or screenshots of what you want · your measurements (bust, waist, hips, height) · a size you own that fits · the occasion · colours you love and avoid · your budget range. Even a mood and a single measurement are enough to begin.

Sizing & fit notes

Vietnamese sizing runs smaller than US/EU and varies by brand, so I do not guess. I match a brand's size chart to your measurements — not to a letter — and confirm real garment measurements when unclear. You will always see photo confirmation before it ships.

Not posted? I can still source it

What you see is a small slice. If a brand sells inside Vietnam, I can usually find it — a boutique's new drop, a restock, a modern áo dài, a vintage piece spotted on TikTok, or a hard-to-find beauty product. Honest boundaries: no counterfeits, no promises on stock that is gone for good, and I confirm price and availability before you pay.
