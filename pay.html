Paying for your order

Nothing is ever paid blind. You approve real photographs of your actual piece first — then your personal payment link arrives inside the conversation we're already having.

When you pay

1
Photo confirmation first.

I find your piece in person and send real photographs. Nothing is purchased until you say yes.

2
50% deposit places the order.

Once you approve, your deposit secures the piece. Processed within 2–7 days. Paying by card? Stripe's processing fee is itemized right on your payment link — transfers skip it.

3
Remaining 50% + shipping before dispatch.

Your exact shipping quote is confirmed before the balance — and I tell you what customs charges to expect for your country, so nothing at the door surprises you.

4
Tracking the moment it ships.

Packed with care in Saigon, tracked worldwide, and I check in when it arrives.

Ways to pay

Wise

Usually the best exchange rate and lowest fees for international transfers.

PayPal

Choose Goods & Services for PayPal's buyer protection on your order.

Payoneer

A reliable option if you already hold a Payoneer balance.

Bank transfer

Direct transfer details are shared in your order thread — Zelle for US clients. No processing fee.

Card · via Stripe

Any major card, on Stripe's own secure page. Adds a ~3.3% processing fee (Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30, passed through at cost and itemized on your payment link). Bank transfer, Wise & Zelle stay fee-free.

USDC · on request

A regulated stablecoin, for clients whose banks make the above difficult.

If none of these reach you easily, tell me your country and I'll suggest a reputable option. Because each order is sourced specifically for you, confirmed purchases are final.

How your payment link arrives

  • Your link or transfer details always arrive inside our existing conversation — the same Instagram DM or email thread where you approved your photos. Never from a new account.
  • The only places I exist: @seraphicstyler on Instagram & TikTok, and seraphicstyler@gmail.com. Treat anything else as an impostor.
  • I will never ask for card numbers, codes, or passwords in a message — payments run through the providers above, on their own secure pages.
